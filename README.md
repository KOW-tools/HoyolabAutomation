# Hoyolab Automation

An Android app to automate your daily HoYoLAB check-ins.

## Supported Games

- **Genshin Impact**
- **Honkai: Star Rail**
- **Zenless Zone Zero**
- **Honkai Impact 3rd**
- **Tears of Themis**

## Features

### Automatic Daily Check-Ins

Set it once and forget it. The app automatically checks in to all your games every day at midnight, collecting your daily rewards without any manual intervention.

### Multi-Game Support

Manage check-ins for all your HoYoverse games from a single app. The app automatically detects which games you play and handles them all.

### Check-In History

View complete logs of all check-ins with detailed status for each game, making it easy to verify your rewards were claimed.

### Notifications

Get notified about your daily check-in results, including success status for each game and any issues that need attention.

## How to Use

### First Time Setup

1. **Install the app** on your Android device (requires Android 10 or higher)

2. **Login to HoYoLAB**
   - Grant the necessary permissions
   - Tap the "Login" button
   - Sign in with your HoYoLAB account credentials

3. **That's it!** The app will automatically:
   - Detect all your linked games
   - Schedule daily check-ins at midnight
   - Send you notifications about the results

### Running Manual Check-In

If you want to check in immediately without waiting for the scheduled time:

1. Open the app
2. Tap the **"Run Now"** button on the home screen
3. Wait for the check-in process to complete
4. Check the notification for results

### Viewing Check-In History

1. Open the app
2. Tap the **logs icon** in the top right corner
3. Browse your check-in history
4. Filter by success/failed status if needed

### Managing Your Account

- **View account info**: Your email and linked games are displayed on the home screen
- **Logout**: Tap your account section and select "Logout" to remove your credentials and stop automatic check-ins

## Notifications

The app sends notifications for:

- Successful check-ins with summary of all games
- Failed check-ins or errors
- Cookie expiration (when you need to login again)
- Network errors

## Troubleshooting

### Cookie expired

When your session expires:
1. You'll receive a notification
2. Open the app
3. Logout and login again to refresh your session

## Privacy & Security

- Your login credentials are securely stored using Android's encrypted storage
- No data is sent to third-party servers
- Check-in logs are stored locally on your device and automatically pruned after 30 days
